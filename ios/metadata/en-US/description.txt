Stock Thesis Ledger is a local-first companion for reproducible stock research and Paper-only review.

Use one shared ledger to:
• refresh timestamped market and SEC evidence;
• inspect deterministic factor scores and data-quality gates;
• record thesis, counter-evidence, invalidation, entry zone, stop, targets, and size;
• explore defined-risk option scenarios;
• prepare day-trade plans with VWAP, opening-range, and no-trade checks; and
• review later outcomes without rewriting the original decision.

The iPhone app connects to a server URL you choose. It shares the same authenticated watchlist, decision history, Paper ledger, and revision history as the Web workspace. Login tokens stay in the device Keychain.

Broker mutations in this build are restricted to Alpaca Paper and remain behind explicit controls and local risk limits. No live brokerage endpoint is implemented.

Stock Thesis Ledger is research and paper-trading software, not investment, legal, tax, or accounting advice. Data can be delayed, incomplete, or indicative. Outputs do not promise returns or instruct you to buy or sell.
