Evidence before action

A thesis you can audit later.

A local-first workspace for stock research, options scenarios, day-trade planning, and Paper-only review. Every decision keeps its evidence, rules, price plan, strategy version, and eventual outcome.

Run locally See the workflow

Sanitized product preview with synthetic data.

Paper-only by construction. No live broker endpoint is implemented. Outputs are inspectable research scenarios, not promises or instructions to trade.
One closed loop

Research, decide, review.

The product is organized around a four-step ledger instead of a feed of opaque signals. A ticker stays attached to the evidence and review that produced the decision.

01 · SELECT

Choose a ticker

Build a focused watchlist and inspect timestamped market and filing coverage.

02 · REFRESH

Refresh evidence

Recompute deterministic factors only when the required source data is ready.

03 · PLAN

Freeze the plan

Record thesis, counter-evidence, entry zone, stop, targets, size, and invalidation.

04 · REVIEW

Grade the outcome

Compare the frozen plan with later bars and Paper execution without rewriting history.

Shared ledger

Web depth. iOS continuity.

The browser and native SwiftUI companion use the same authenticated SQLite ledger, revision cursor, strategy context, and Paper history.

Day Trade

Market clock, VWAP, opening range, no-trade gates, risk capacity, replay, and review stay separate from long-term scoring.

Options Lab

Indicative chains, liquidity, Greeks, one-to-six-leg payoff scenarios, defined-risk filters, and Paper review.

Trust boundary

Deterministic core, visible limits.

The server owns calculations and mutation validation. AI is not required. Missing or stale evidence blocks actionable classifications instead of being hidden by a confident explanation.

Local-first

One Python standard-library server and one SQLite database on a machine you control. No cloud database or paid app backend is required.

Versioned reasoning

Decision inputs, factor weights, model version, strategy context, evidence, counter-evidence, and invalidation remain replayable.

Paper controls

Alpaca mutations are fixed to paper-api.alpaca.markets and require enablement, acknowledgements, local limits, and idempotent IDs.

Forward validation

A 30–60 day campaign tracks coverage, resolved outcomes, plan reviews, intraday sessions, blockers, and protocol deviations.

Architecture

Small enough to understand.

A modular monolith keeps synchronization and risk rules in one place while Web and iOS remain focused clients.
