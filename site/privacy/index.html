Privacy

Data stays with the server you choose.

Effective August 26, 2026 · Applies to the open-source v0.1.6 reference build.

Who operates the data

The open-source maintainer does not operate a hosted Stock Thesis Ledger service and does not receive data from the reference build. You choose and operate the server URL. If another person or organization operates that server, ask that operator how it handles your data.

Data handled by the app

The selected server stores account email, password verifier, preferences, watchlists, research records, Paper plans and orders, journal entries, device registrations, and sync history. The iOS app stores its server URL, language and notification preferences in UserDefaults and its bearer token in the device Keychain.

External providers

The local server may send ticker symbols and API credentials to providers you configure, including Alpha Vantage, SEC EDGAR, and Alpaca Paper/IEX. Their policies govern that processing. Broker mutations are restricted to Alpaca Paper in this build.

Retention and deletion

Records remain on the selected server until its operator deletes them. In the iOS app, open Settings → Danger zone → Delete account and all records to request deletion from that server and sign out connected devices. Local app data can also be removed by deleting the app.

Contact

For a security issue, follow the private reporting process in the project’s SECURITY.md. For other questions, use the support page.
