## Paper-only brokerage boundary

The distributed project implements Alpaca Paper order workflows and fixes the
trading host to `paper-api.alpaca.markets`. It does not implement live-account
brokerage routing. Paper fills, balances, buying power, fees, and market impact
do not necessarily match a live account.
